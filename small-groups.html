Small Groups

Life Change Happens in Small Groups

We believe life is best shared, discipleship best formed, and leaders best raised in the soil of small groups — where members are known, shepherded, and sent out to serve.

Rather than build separate ministries and silos, our whole community centers on small groups: real people, meeting regularly, growing together. As groups multiply, new leaders rise up to shepherd them — and the whole body grows, in maturity and in number.

Email hello@sequoiacommunitychurch.org and we'll connect you with a group.

Why Small Groups

Known. Shepherded. Sent.

Known

Real relationships, not just a seat in a room — groups are where people actually get to know each other.

Shepherded

Every group has care and leadership close by, not distant — discipleship happens person to person.

Sent

As groups grow, new leaders rise up to lead new groups — multiplication, not just addition.
