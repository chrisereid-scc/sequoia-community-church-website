Welcome Home to Sequoia Community Church

Care. Community. Discipleship. Authentic.

Sequoia Community Church is a church rooted in authentic relationships, united and strengthened in community, transformed through discipleship, and shepherded with genuine care. We grow together the way a sequoia grows — not alone, but in a grove, roots intertwined, holding one another up.
