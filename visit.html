What to Expect

First time? Here's what our rhythm looks like. Most months we gather six times: three Sunday services and three small group evenings — with one week off each month to rest as families and as a community.

  • When Sundays at 10:30am
  • Where Email us for the address →
  • What to wear Come as you are — there's no dress code here.
  • Kids Childcare is provided during our gathering. Please let us know you're coming so we know how many little ones to expect.
  • How long Services run about 90 minutes.
